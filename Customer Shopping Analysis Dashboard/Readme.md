# Customer Analysis Dashboard

An interactive Excel dashboard project created to analyze customer purchasing behavior, revenue trends, and shopping patterns across multiple shopping malls.

The dashboard was built using Pivot Tables, Pivot Charts, Slicers, and Excel data visualization techniques to transform raw transaction data into actionable business insights.

## Dataset Overview

The dataset contains:

* 99,457 customer transactions
* Customer demographics (Age, Gender)
* Product categories
* Payment methods
* Shopping mall locations
* Invoice and purchase information
* Revenue and sales data

---

## Key Insights

* Female customers contributed a larger share of total revenue than male customers.
* Clothing generated the highest revenue among all product categories.
* Cash was the most preferred payment method, followed by credit cards.
* Kanyon and Mall of Istanbul were the top-performing shopping malls by revenue.
* Revenue was highest at the beginning of the year and showed a gradual decline over subsequent months.
* Customers aged 18–25 represented the largest customer segment.
